# Google Play listing draft

## App name

UFI Phone Companion

## Short description

Calls and SMS from a compatible UFI modem on your private local network.

## Full description

UFI Phone Companion turns a supported USB Wi-Fi modem into a simple local
phone and messaging gateway. Connect your Android device to the modem's Wi-Fi,
pair it once, and use a familiar Calls, Keypad, and Messages interface.

Features:

- receive and place ordinary carrier calls;
- answer calls and carry two-way audio through your Android device;
- read, reply to, and send SMS directly through the modem;
- see local call history and unread-message badges;
- monitor whether the modem is ready for incoming calls;
- keep call audio and message content on your private modem LAN.

Important compatibility information:

UFI Phone Companion is not a standalone mobile-phone service. It requires a
separately prepared, compatible UFI modem running the open-source UFI Phone
gateway. Hardware sold as “UFI” uses many unrelated chipsets and firmware
builds; check the compatibility list before installing. The currently tested
UFI003 uses 2G/3G circuit-switched fallback for voice and does not provide
native IMS/VoLTE. Carrier charges may apply.

Emergency numbers, supplementary service codes, and guaranteed background
operation on unsupported devices are intentionally not provided. Telegram is
not required and is disabled by default.

Source, setup guide, and compatibility list:
https://github.com/Ruskuv1708/ufi-sms-telegram-forwarder
